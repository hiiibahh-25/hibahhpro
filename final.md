# 💎 Zomurda – Women's Freelancing Platform

### Empowering Women Through Digital Opportunities

## 1. Project Overview

Zomurda is a digital freelancing platform designed to empower women with digital skills by connecting them with project owners, startups, and businesses seeking professional digital services.

The platform aims to create a safe, flexible, and professional environment where women can showcase their talents, access opportunities, and participate in the digital freelance market.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many women with digital skills face challenges accessing freelance opportunities, reaching potential clients, and presenting their professional capabilities in a trusted digital environment.

Zomurda addresses this need by providing a dedicated platform that connects women professionals with businesses and project owners.

## 3. Target Audience

- Women freelancers with digital skills.
- Entrepreneurs and project owners.
- Startups and businesses seeking digital services.

## 4. Proposed Solution

Zomurda provides a digital platform that brings together female freelancers and clients through a professional environment for discovering skills and digital services.
