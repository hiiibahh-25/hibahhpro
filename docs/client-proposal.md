# Zomurda – Client Proposal

## 1. Project Background

Zomurda is a digital freelancing platform designed to empower women with digital skills by connecting them with clients, entrepreneurs, and startups seeking professional digital services.

The idea emerged from recognizing the challenges women face in accessing freelance opportunities, reaching potential clients, and presenting their skills in a professional digital environment.

## 2. Problem Statement

Women with technical and creative digital skills may struggle to find suitable freelance opportunities and connect with potential clients.

Additionally, platform commissions can reduce freelancers' earnings and influence their willingness to use online freelancing platforms.

This creates a need for a dedicated platform that supports women's professional growth and provides better access to digital opportunities.

## 3. Problem Research – Survey

To better understand the problem, a questionnaire was conducted targeting female engineers and colleagues.

The survey aimed to explore opinions about freelance platform commissions and how these fees affect women's willingness to use freelancing platforms.

### Key Finding

**60% of respondents supported the idea of a freelancing platform that does not deduct commissions from freelancers' earnings.**

This finding provided evidence of interest in the proposed concept and supported the decision to develop Zomurda.

*Note: This percentage represents the actual survey result. It should not be interpreted as a general statistic about all women freelancers.*

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary Users
- Women with programming and web development skills.
- Women working in AI and automation.
- Graphic designers and creative professionals.
- Digital project management specialists.

### Secondary Users
- Entrepreneurs.
- Startup founders.
- Small businesses.
- Project owners seeking digital services.

## 5. Proposed Solution

Zomurda is a digital platform that connects women with digital skills to potential clients and businesses through a dedicated professional freelancing environment.

The platform aims to make discovering talent and digital services easier while helping women showcase their capabilities and access new professional opportunities.

## 6. Key Services

- **Programming & Web Development:** Building websites and digital solutions.
- **Artificial Intelligence & Automation:** AI-powered solutions and workflow automation.
- **Graphic Design & Creative Services:** Visual design and creative digital content.
- **Digital Project Management:** Organizing and managing digital projects.

## 7. Expected Value

Zomurda aims to:

- Improve women's access to freelance opportunities.
- Provide a professional space to showcase digital skills.
- Connect businesses with qualified female talent.
- Reduce barriers between freelancers and potential clients.
- Support women's participation in the digital economy.

## 8. Project Vision

To build a trusted digital environment where women's talents become visible, their skills create opportunities, and their work contributes to the growing digital economy.
